AR Furniture TryOn Suite

Markerless Surface Detection & Precision Spatial Anchoring

This Unity Augmented Reality project allows users to place virtual 3D furniture models onto horizontal planes in real-world environments. Utilizing AR Foundation, raycasting plane detection, and physical scale calibration, the app enables seamless visualization before purchasing.

<KEY ARCHITECTURAL FEATURES />

  • AR Foundation Surface Detection
  • Real-time Model Scaling & Rotation
  • Real-World Dynamic Shadows
  • Texture & Color Material Swapping
  • High Performance Mobile Shaders
